About Us

We are MoonLab AI, a team of six students passionate about space science. We created “Abandoned but not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA’s Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars” for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 – Ibra.

Our idea

Old spacecraft are not just scrap. Each one carries a mission, a team and a discovery. We turn them into Interactive Graphic Novel chapters, with Audio Drama and cinematic effects, so anyone, from beginners to space fans, can learn through story.
